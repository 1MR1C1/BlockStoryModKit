# Block Story Mod Kit

Launcher and modding kit for Block Story, on Windows and Linux. Play with mods, grab mods other people made, or make your own without setting anything up first.

It works with BlockStoryCore, the in-game mod framework. If you just want to play with mods, download a release and point it at your game.

## Download

Grab the build for your system from [Releases](../../releases):

- Windows: `BlockStoryModKit-Windows.exe`
- Linux: `BlockStoryModKit-Linux`

Nothing else to install, just run it.

## What's in it

- **Launcher** - finds your game and lists your mods. Turn mods on and off, install one someone sent you (a .dll, a .zip or a folder), remove them, and save profiles so you can swap between mod sets. Launches the game through Steam and tells you when mods or the app have updates. Backup / Load saves your whole setup.
- **Mod store** - browse, install and rate mods, follow authors, and upload your own.
- **Mod Builder** - pick a starter template (panel, settings page, keybind, Harmony patch, block watcher, wild creature, rideable pet and more), then build and install it in one go. Your mods are listed so you can edit, rebuild or delete them.
- **Game API** - search the game's classes, methods, fields and item/block names while you write a mod.
- **Setup & Help** - one click framework install, guides, a bug report button, and a quick status check of your game, mods and .NET.

A few looks to pick from in your profile: Fantasy (default), Slate, Charcoal and Neon.

## Requirements

- Block Story (not included).
- The mod framework, which the launcher installs for you.
- The .NET 8 SDK, only if you want to build mods yourself.
